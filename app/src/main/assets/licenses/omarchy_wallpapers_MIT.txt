The 6 wallpaper images bundled with this app (res/drawable-nodpi/wallpaper_*.webp) are
downscaled and recompressed from the Omarchy project's per-theme backgrounds:
https://github.com/basecamp/omarchy/tree/master/themes/<theme>/backgrounds/

tokyo_night    <- themes/tokyo-night/backgrounds/0-liftoff.webp
gruvbox        <- themes/gruvbox/backgrounds/1-the-backwater.jpg
nord           <- themes/nord/backgrounds/1-city-view.webp
catppuccin     <- themes/catppuccin/backgrounds/2-waves.webp
everforest     <- themes/everforest/backgrounds/1-tree-tops.webp
matte_black    <- themes/matte-black/backgrounds/1-dark-waters.webp
